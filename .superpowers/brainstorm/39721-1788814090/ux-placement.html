Curated Precedents & Peer Benchmarks: User Experience & Placement

Where and how should analysts interact with this data?

A

In-Situ Integration (Directly on Existing Charts & Labs)

Add a "Precedent Overlay" toggle on existing charts (e.g. on Global Liquidity, Energy Regime, India Macro). Clicking "Compare vs 2008 GFC" or "Peer Benchmark: G20 Median" directly overlays the normalized historical path onto the current chart.

Best for seamless workflow without leaving the current dashboard view.
B

Dedicated "Macro Precedent & Benchmarks" Lab (/labs/precedents)

A dedicated workspace where analysts can pick a current indicator (e.g. US Net Liquidity, India RBI FX reserves) and select a benchmark peer group or historical analog library (1970s Stagflation, 1997 AFC, 2013 Taper Tantrum) with a normalized T=0 timeline.

Best for deep-dive regime research and institutional report generation.
C

Hybrid (Quick Glance Chips in Terminal + Full Dedicated Lab)

On Metric Cards/Charts, show a small badge like "Analog: 2013 Taper Tantrum (84% correlation)" or "Vs G20: 88th percentile" that links out to the dedicated deep-dive Lab.

Recommended: Preserves terminal cleanliness while offering institutional depth.
