Macro vs. Benchmarks / Historical Precedents

What problem does this solve for a macro analyst?

Right now GraphiQuestor shows raw telemetry — a metric's current value, its freshness, and its time-series. But a number in isolation has limited signal. Context is where the intelligence lives.

"Global M2 is $104T" means nothing alone. But "Global M2 is in the 94th percentile of its 20-year range and last time it was this high, EM risk assets repriced -30% over 18 months" — that's actionable intelligence.

Three distinct value propositions

A

Percentile Rank + Historical Range

Where does the current reading sit in its own history? Show a 10/25/75/90th percentile band so the analyst can see at a glance whether conditions are extreme, normal, or anomalous. Think: a thermometer, not just a number.

B

Historical Analog / Precedent Matching

Find prior episodes where multiple macro indicators had a similar configuration (e.g. high inflation + inverted yield curve + tightening liquidity) and show what happened next. A "playbook" of similar historical regimes.

C

Cross-Country / Cross-Asset Benchmark

How does India's credit growth compare to G20 peers at similar stages of their cycle? How does current oil-gold ratio compare to prior commodity super-cycles? Peer and cross-asset relativization.

Select one or more that resonate. These can be built independently — they're not all-or-nothing.
