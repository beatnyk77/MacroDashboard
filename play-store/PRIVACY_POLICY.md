GraphiQuestor ("we", "our", or "the Service") operates the GraphiQuestor web application and the GraphiQuestor Native Android application. This Privacy Policy informs users of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when using our service and the choices associated with that data.

---

## 1. Information We Do NOT Collect
GraphiQuestor is an institutional macro intelligence terminal engineered on a **data-minimization philosophy**. We do not collect:
* Personal identifying information (such as real name, physical address, or phone number).
* Financial account details, bank credentials, brokerage accounts, or credit card numbers.
* Biometric data, health information, contacts, or device media.
* Precise or approximate geographic location.

## 2. On-Device Local Data Storage
To provide instantaneous offline performance and customize your experience, the Android application stores the following information locally on your device using a local Room SQLite database:
* Pinned metrics and custom watchlist configurations.
* User-defined alert threshold rules (e.g. Reverse Repo drain triggers).
* Cached statistical observations for offline charting.

**This data remains strictly on your local device.** It is not sold, rented, or transmitted to third-party marketing companies. You can delete this data at any time by clearing the application's storage in your Android device settings.

## 3. Network Communications & Data in Transit
All network requests from the mobile application to our edge infrastructure (Cloudflare CDN and Supabase backend) are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Requests retrieve public macroeconomic indicators and statistical releases published by governmental and monetary authorities (e.g., Federal Reserve H.4.1, US Department of the Treasury, Reserve Bank of India, EIA).

## 4. Third-Party Services
The Android application utilizes the following trusted infrastructure partners strictly for performance, stability, and notification routing:
* **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)**: To deliver server-side push notifications when systemic macro thresholds are breached. FCM uses an ephemeral instance token to route alerts.
* **Google Play In-App Review API**: To allow users to submit app ratings directly through Google Play services without harvesting external identifiers.

## 5. Advertising and Tracking
GraphiQuestor contains **zero advertisements** and incorporates **zero third-party behavioral advertising trackers** (e.g., no Google AdMob, no Meta Audience Network). We do not collect or track Google Advertising IDs (AAID).

## 6. Financial Disclaimer
GraphiQuestor is a pure telemetry terminal providing macroeconomic and central bank data for informational and research purposes only. GraphiQuestor is not a registered broker-dealer, investment adviser, or commodities trading advisor. Nothing in the application constitutes financial, investment, legal, or tax advice.

## 7. Children's Privacy
Our Service is directed at financial professionals and adults and does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
